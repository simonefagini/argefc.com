ARGE FC

Arge /ˈaʁɡə/ noun

Abb. of Arbeitsgemeinschaft: a working group formed around a shared objective.

Arge FC is a football club founded in 2021 by an association of small architecture offices with only one shared goal: to compete in the Basel S AM Cup and “beat the big ones!”.

As of 2026, it is formed by Guerra ClaussFM, an-a-cFM, SUPERFLUOFM, Kooperative E45, Studio Bosco Ferreira, Diwersy Weimar, Office for Ordinary Objects (ooo), Atelier Neume, Studio Lukas Kerner, Studio Hammer, Studio copan, and Weyell Zipse.
